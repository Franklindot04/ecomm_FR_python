# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. All contributors, maintainers, and community participants are expected to interact professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Providing constructive and actionable feedback.
* Assuming good intent while addressing disagreements professionally.
* Focusing on the technical discussion and the quality of the project.
* Welcoming questions and different levels of experience.
* Giving appropriate credit to the work and ideas of others.
* Accepting feedback and correction in a professional manner.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, insulting, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
* Publishing private or sensitive information about another person without permission.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions or project activities.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional in a technical community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, including removing content, restricting participation, or taking other actions they consider appropriate to protect the project community.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the appropriate contact method listed in the repository or project profile.

Please provide enough context to help the maintainers understand what happened. Reports will be reviewed as seriously and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions where an individual is representing or participating in the project.

## Acknowledgement

This Code of Conduct is intended to support a respectful and constructive open-source community. It may be updated as the project and its community evolve.
